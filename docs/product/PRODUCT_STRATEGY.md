# Course Manager Product Strategy (Enterprise-First)

## Document Purpose
Define product direction after strategic evaluation of two options:
1. Build a large consumer learning marketplace (Coursera/LinkedIn Learning model).
2. Focus on enterprise learning and workforce enablement.

Decision: **Enterprise-first B2B strategy**.

## Strategic Context
Course Manager operates in a crowded online learning market where consumer distribution, brand, and content scale are expensive moats to build. Enterprise learning demand remains durable due to:
- AI-driven role change and ongoing reskilling pressure.
- Budget ownership and renewals tied to workforce outcomes.
- Greater willingness to pay for integration, governance, and measurable business impact.

Recent public market signals from major learning platforms show stronger enterprise performance than consumer segments, reinforcing enterprise as the better fit for durable growth and monetization.

## Strategy Statement
Course Manager will become the **skills operating system for mid-market and enterprise organizations**, helping leaders identify skill gaps, assign targeted learning, and prove workforce impact.

## Product Principles
1. **Outcomes over content volume**: prioritize measurable skill and business outcomes over catalog size.
2. **Admin-first usability**: make setup, governance, and rollout fast for L&D and HR teams.
3. **Integrate into existing stack**: reduce adoption friction with SSO/SCIM, HRIS, and LMS/LXP interoperability.
4. **Manager-in-the-loop**: enable team leads to drive learning plans, not just individual learners.
5. **Trust by design**: enterprise-grade permissions, auditability, and compliance from the start.

## Target Customers
- Primary: mid-market and enterprise organizations (500-10,000 employees) with active reskilling initiatives.
- Economic buyers: Chief Learning Officer, VP HR, Head of Talent, L&D Director.
- Power users: L&D admins, HR business partners, department managers.
- End users: employees with role-based learning goals.

## Core Jobs To Be Done
- Identify critical skill gaps by role/team.
- Assign and track role-specific learning paths.
- Connect learning activity to workforce and business outcomes.
- Operate learning programs securely at scale.

## Value Proposition
Course Manager helps enterprises close workforce skill gaps faster by combining:
- skill intelligence,
- role-aligned pathway assignment,
- and executive-ready ROI analytics.

## Business Model Direction
- Subscription SaaS by active learners/admins with tiered feature gates.
- Enterprise add-ons: advanced analytics, premium integrations, governance/compliance features.
- Land-and-expand motion: department pilot -> enterprise rollout.

## 12-Month Strategic Objectives
1. Establish enterprise product foundation for onboarding and governance.
2. Launch role-based skills intelligence and pathway workflows.
3. Deliver executive ROI analytics that supports renewals and expansion.

## North Star And Key Metrics
- **North Star**: Monthly Active Learners with Assigned Role-Based Pathways.
- Product metrics:
  - Time-to-first-value (days from contract to first assignment).
  - Assignment completion rate.
  - Skill-gap coverage (% workforce with mapped role skills).
  - Manager engagement (monthly active managers).
  - Admin weekly active rate.
- Business metrics:
  - Gross and net revenue retention.
  - Expansion ARR.
  - Renewal rate.

## Scope Boundaries (What We Will Not Prioritize Now)
- Building a broad open consumer marketplace.
- Heavy investment in consumer creator monetization.
- B2C growth loops as primary go-to-market.

## Strategic Risks And Mitigations
- Risk: slower enterprise sales cycles.
  - Mitigation: pilot package with fast onboarding and clear 90-day success criteria.
- Risk: integration complexity delays value.
  - Mitigation: staged integration roadmap and standardized implementation playbooks.
- Risk: inability to prove business impact.
  - Mitigation: ship ROI dashboard and baseline measurement framework early.

## Go-To-Market Alignment
- Product-led pilot for one business unit.
- Standardized implementation for first 30/60/90 days.
- QBR-ready reporting to support renewal and expansion decisions.
